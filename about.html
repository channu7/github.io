About

I have six years of research experience designing and developing machine learning methods, first as a Master's student and then as a research assistant and project associate. My background covers microscopic imaging, image processing and natural language processing.

I have published 21 papers, 5 of them as first author. Two of these received Best Paper awards (IOCA 2021 and ICIEA 2022). My work includes BCNet, a deep learning framework for peripheral blood cell identification, and FMDNet, a lightweight face mask detection model.

I enjoy writing manuscripts and research proposals, planning independent research, and advising other group members. I am committed to computer vision and want to contribute work that benefits people in practice. I am looking for a role in a professional, learning-oriented environment where innovation and results are the measure of success.
