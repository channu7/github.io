Welcome

I am a PhD research scholar in Computer Science at the University of Mysore. My thesis work is on IoMT-based diagnostic and point-of-care systems, and more broadly I design deep learning methods for image-based problems: microscopic and medical imaging, agriculture and food quality, and robotic grasping with reinforcement learning.

Before the PhD I worked at CSIR–CFTRI (Mysuru), Kyung Hee University (Republic of Korea) and IIIT Kottayam. I also teach, and have been a visiting professor at Huanghuai University and Xuchang University in China.
